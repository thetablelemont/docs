publish: true
---

**Before service**
- [ ] Service plan loaded in ProPresenter
- [ ] Clicked through every song once
- [ ] Know any changes to song order (repeats, skips)
- [ ] Today's video plays, with sound
- [ ] Reviewed sermon slides with the pastor

**During service**
- [ ] **First worship slide** starts the service (lights, camera, looks)
- [ ] Follow the worship leader, not the slide order
- [ ] Wrong slide? Click to the right one. No big deal.
- [ ] **First sermon slide** switches to the message look

**End**
- [ ] **Last slide** ends the livestream. Only when service is over.
