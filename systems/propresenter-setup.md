ProPresenter runs more than slides at The Table. Macros attached to slides change the lights, looks, camera and confidence monitor all at once. That's why the slide operator's clicks matter.

## On startup

ProPresenter opens and runs the **worship macro** on its own. When the camera picture shows up, everything has loaded.

## Macros on first slides

The first slide of each kind of presentation carries a macro:

- **The first slide of a song** sets the worship lights, look and confidence monitor layout.
- **The first sermon slide** sets the message look.
- **The last slide of the service** ends the livestream.

Clicking those slides is what changes the room. Clicking a slide early changes the room early.

## Where the plan comes from

ProPresenter pulls each service plan from [[planning-center|Planning Center]]. Song slides come from the ProPresenter library, and sermon slides are built each week. See [[sermon-slides|Sermon slides]].
