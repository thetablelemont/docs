Audio runs the Behringer XR18 mixer from the Mixing Station app. Your job: everyone on stage hears what they need, and the room hears a clear, even mix.

## Before you start

- The system is on. See [[power-on-and-off|Power on and off]].
- Open **Mixing Station** on the church iPad and select **The Table XR18**.
- Unmute the bass channel. It's left muted between services.
- Get the latest channel presets on the iPad. See [[mixing-station|Mixing Station presets]].

## Line check

Do this at the start of every rehearsal, Wednesday and Sunday, even when the mixer is already in a good spot.

1. Go input by input: each mic, instrument and playback source.
2. Have each person play or sing at performance volume.
3. **Gain first.** Set it so the meter sits in the green-to-yellow range. See [[audio-basics#Gain staging|Gain staging]].
4. Then EQ, compression and monitor sends, only where needed.
5. Ask each musician whether they can hear themselves and what they need.

## During the service

- Watch the pastor's mic and the worship leader's vocal most closely. Those are the two that matter most to the room.
- Make small moves. If something sounds wrong, check gain before reaching for EQ.

## Gear you'll touch

- XR18 mixer, controlled over Wi-Fi from Mixing Station
- Shure BLX wireless with PG58 mics (rechargeable AA batteries, kept on the charger)
- Two Behringer Eurolive F1220D stage wedges
- Roland V-Drums and Roland RD-88 keyboard
- More in [[gear|Gear]]
