---

**Stage**
- [ ] Two stage wedges on
- [ ] Three stage lamps on
- [ ] V-Drums on
- [ ] RD-88 keyboard on
- [ ] Bass amp on
- [ ] Charged batteries in every wireless mic

**Back of room**
- [ ] Power amp on (room speakers)
- [ ] Sign in to the Mac mini
- [ ] Lightkey → Sunday service → double-click **All On**
- [ ] ProPresenter open. Wait for the camera picture.
- [ ] Click a slide so the screens aren't black
- [ ] Audience TVs on (2)
- [ ] Confidence monitor on

**Mixer**
- [ ] Mixing Station → The Table XR18
- [ ] Unmute bass
- [ ] Line check every input

**Before 9:45**
- [ ] Today's video plays, with sound
- [ ] Pastor has checked his slides and mic

**After service (power off)**
- [ ] Power amp off
- [ ] Drums and keys off
- [ ] Mute bass channel, **then** bass amp off
- [ ] Stage lamps off
- [ ] Mic batteries on the charger
- [ ] Quit Lightkey, then ProPresenter
- [ ] TVs off
