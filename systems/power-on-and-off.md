Power on in this order: stage first, then the amplifier, then the computer. Power off in reverse. Turning the amp on last and off first keeps loud pops out of the speakers.

## Power on

### 1. Stage

- [ ] Two stage wedges (Behringer Eurolive F1220D): switch on the back
- [ ] Three lamp lights on stage
- [ ] Roland V-Drums
- [ ] Roland RD-88 keyboard
- [ ] Bass amp
- [ ] Fresh AA batteries in every wireless mic (Shure BLX / PG58)

### 2. Amplifier (back of the room)

- [ ] Behringer power amp. This turns on the four room speakers: two mains by the stage and two delays farther down the room.

### 3. Computer

- [ ] Sign in to the Mac mini at the back
- [ ] Open **Lightkey** → **Sunday service** project → double-click **All On**
- [ ] Open **ProPresenter**. It runs the worship macro on startup. **When the camera picture appears, ProPresenter is ready.**
- [ ] Click the first worship slide (or any slide) so the screens have something on them

### 4. Screens

- [ ] Two audience TVs
- [ ] Confidence monitor at the back of the room

### 5. Mixer

- [ ] Open Mixing Station on the iPad → **The Table XR18** → unmute bass → [[audio#Line check|Line check]]

Everything is on. Start line check.

## Power off

> [!note] Confirm with Keith
> This section is the standard reverse order. It hasn't been checked against how we actually shut down.

1. Turn the **power amp** off first.
2. Turn off the TVs and confidence monitor.
3. Quit ProPresenter and Lightkey.
4. Turn off the stage: wedges, drums, keyboard, bass amp, lamps.
5. Take the batteries out of the wireless mics. Recharge them if they're rechargeable.
