The mixer starts each week from a good baseline. Saved channel presets get each singer and instrument most of the way there before line check. The problem is that a preset saved on one device doesn't show up on the others by itself.

## Where the presets live

- **Master copy:** the tech director's phone. That's where most mixing happens and where presets are kept up to date.
- **Church iPad:** what everyone else uses. Bring it up to date before you mix.

## Two kinds of saved settings

- **Channel presets** hold one person's settings: gain, EQ, compression, sends. In Mixing Station, open the channel, tap the **Folder** icon, then **+** to save or **Load** to recall.
- **Scenes** hold the whole mixer. Open the main menu, tap **Show**, then **+** to save or **Load** to recall.

## Getting the latest onto the iPad

We move presets with a backup file and AirDrop. No shared login needed.

1. **On the master phone:** open Mixing Station, tap the gear icon on the start screen → **Backup / Restore** → back up to a file.
2. **AirDrop** the file to the church iPad.
3. **On the iPad:** gear icon → **Backup / Restore** → restore from that file.

Do this whenever presets change. The week of a new player or a big mix change is a good time.

> [!warning] Restoring replaces the iPad's settings
> Back up the iPad first if it has anything you want to keep.
