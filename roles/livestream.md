We stream Sunday service to YouTube at [youtube.com/@thetablelemont](https://youtube.com/@thetablelemont). ProPresenter starts and ends the stream. The only weekly job is scheduling it.

## During the week (5 minutes)

1. Open [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UCyU7upEfTQI8xVKt1KG1fUQ/livestreaming/manage) and sign in with the church account.
2. Click **Create**, then **Go live**.
3. Choose **Reuse settings** to copy last week's setup.
4. Change the title to this Sunday's date.
5. Set the time to 10:00 AM and click **Done**.

## On Sunday

- ProPresenter starts streaming at 10:00 on its own.
- The **first worship slide** switches the stream to the stage camera.
- The **last slide** ends the stream.

## Cameras

Two AVKANS LV20 PTZ cameras: the main stage shot and a side angle. See [[video-routing|Video routing]].

## When it breaks

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stream doesn't start | In YouTube Studio, check that the event is scheduled for today |
| Stream ended early | The last slide was clicked too soon. Start a new stream from YouTube Studio. |
| No video on stream | Check ProPresenter's stream output settings |
| Choppy video | Close other apps on the Mac mini and check the network |
