Power on in this order: stage first, then the amplifier, then the computer. The power amp goes on last and off first, which keeps loud pops out of the speakers.

## Power on

### 1. Stage

- [ ] Two stage wedges (Behringer Eurolive F1220D): switch on the back
- [ ] Three lamp lights on stage
- [ ] Roland V-Drums VAD504
- [ ] Roland RD-88 keyboard
- [ ] Bass amp
- [ ] Charged batteries in every wireless mic (Shure BLX / PG58), straight from the charger

### 2. Amplifier (back of the room)

- [ ] Behringer Europower EP2000 power amp. This turns on the four room speakers: two mains by the stage and two delays farther down the room.

### 3. Computer

- [ ] Sign in to the Mac mini at the back
- [ ] Open **Lightkey** → **Sunday service** project → double-click **All On**
- [ ] Open **ProPresenter**. It runs the worship macro on startup. **When the camera picture appears, ProPresenter is ready.**
- [ ] Click the first worship slide (or any slide) so the screens have something on them

### 4. Screens

- [ ] Two audience TVs
- [ ] Confidence monitor at the back of the room

### 5. Mixer

- [ ] Open Mixing Station on the iPad → **The Table XR18** → unmute bass → [[audio#Line check|Line check]]

Everything is on. Start line check.

## Power off

Speakers go off first so nothing pops. Mute the bass channel **before** turning off the bass amp, or it buzzes.

1. **Power amp off** (room speakers)
2. **Drums and keyboard off**
3. **Mute the bass channel** in Mixing Station, then turn off the **bass amp**
4. **Stage lamps off**
5. **Mic batteries out.** They're rechargeable, so put them back on the charger.
6. **Quit Lightkey**
7. **Quit ProPresenter**
8. **TVs off**: audience TVs and confidence monitor
