How sound, screens, lights and the livestream run at The Table, and how to help.

Everything here is written for someone new. Start with the week, pick a role, and use the links when you want to go deeper.

## The week

Sunday is the finish line. Most of the work happens before it.

- **[[monday-prep|Monday]]**: check the plan in Planning Center, load it into ProPresenter, prep anything physical.
- **[[wednesday-rehearsal|Wednesday]]**: the stage is on and ready by 6pm, we connect, line check, rehearse, and finish by 7:30.
- **[[sermon-slides|Thursday–Friday]]**: the pastor's manuscript becomes slides. Finished before Sunday, always.
- **[[sunday-morning|Sunday]]**: 8:30 call, power on, rehearse until 9:30, pray at 9:45, start around 10:05.

## Roles

Each role page says what you do, when, and what "ready" looks like.

- **[[audio|Audio]]**: run the XR18 from Mixing Station. Line check, gain, monitor mixes.
- **[[slides|Slides]]**: run ProPresenter. Your clicks also change the lights and run the livestream.
- **[[lights|Lights]]**: mostly automatic. Lightkey follows ProPresenter.
- **[[livestream|Livestream]]**: YouTube, scheduled during the week, started and ended by ProPresenter.
