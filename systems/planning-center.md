Planning Center is the source of truth for every service: who's serving, the order of service, songs and notes. If it's not in Planning Center, it isn't planned.

Main page: [thetablechurchlemont.churchcenter.com](https://thetablechurchlemont.churchcenter.com)

## For volunteers

**Check your schedule:**

1. Download the **Church Center** app.
2. Search for **The Table Church Lemont** and sign in with your email.
3. Tap **My Schedule**.

**Accept or decline:** open the email or notification and tap **Accept** or **Decline**. If you decline, add a short note. Please reply within 48 hours.

Sunday Service teams: Vocalists, Band, Tech, Speakers, Hospitality, Facility.

**Add a song to a plan:** open the plan → **Add Item** → search for the song → drag it into place.

**Schedule someone:** open the plan → click the open position → search for the person → **Schedule**. They'll get an email to accept.
