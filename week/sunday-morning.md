Call time is 8:30. Service starts around 10:05.

## Timeline

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 8:15 | Ideal arrival for tech |
| 8:30 | Everyone arrives. Tech is powering on. |
| 8:30–9:30 | Line check, then rehearse what we worked on Wednesday |
| 9:30–9:45 | Pastor checks slides and mic. Last fixes. |
| 9:45 | Team prays together in the side nursery room and talks through the service |
| 10:00 | On stage |
| ~10:05 | Service starts with the first worship slide |

## Power on

Follow [[power-on-and-off|Power on and off]] or the printed [[sunday-power-on-checklist|checklist]]. When ProPresenter shows the camera feed, the system is up.

## Before rehearsal

- Line check every input, even though the mixer starts from a good baseline. Adjust gain, EQ and monitor sends as people need. See [[audio#Line check|Line check]].
- If there's a video today, play it through once. Check that the picture looks right and the audio comes through the speakers.

## Pastor check (9:30–9:45)

- The pastor reviews his slides in ProPresenter. Make any edits together with the slide operator.
- He checks his own mic. It stays at the same setting every week, so this is just "does it work".

## During service

- **First worship slide:** click it to start. It changes the lights, looks and camera all at once. The slide operator should be ready for it.
- **Everything in between** runs from the slides as built.
- **Last slide:** ends the livestream. Click it only when the service is actually over.

## After service

Follow the power-off section of [[power-on-and-off|Power on and off]].
