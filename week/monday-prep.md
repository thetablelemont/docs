Monday sets up the week. By the end of it, the next service exists in ProPresenter and anything physical is on the list.

## What happens

1. **Open the plan in [[planning-center|Planning Center]].** It is the source of truth for every service: who's serving, the order of service, songs, and notes.
2. **Check the worship set.** The worship director enters the songs. If they're not in yet, work on everything else and come back.
3. **Load the plan into ProPresenter.** ProPresenter pulls the plan from Planning Center. Look over every song, slide and media item.
4. **Fill gaps.** Add anything missing in Planning Center or ProPresenter: new songs, announcements, videos, backgrounds.
5. **Note physical prep.** Anything that needs setting up, moving, buying or fixing before Wednesday.

## Done when

- The next service plan is loaded in ProPresenter.
- Every song in the set has slides.
- Videos for the week are in and [[troubleshooting#Video won't play or has no sound|tested]].
- Physical prep is listed and has an owner.
