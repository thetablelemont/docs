Slides runs ProPresenter on the Mac mini at the back. Your clicks do more than change words on a screen. They also change the lights, the camera and the livestream. See [[propresenter-setup|ProPresenter setup]].

## Before the service

- Make sure the service plan is loaded. It comes from [[planning-center|Planning Center]].
- Click through every song and the sermon once.
- If there's a video, play it once and check the sound. See [[troubleshooting#Video won't play or has no sound|Video won't play]].
- With the pastor, review the sermon slides before 9:45.

## Song order

The band doesn't always play songs the way the recording does. We repeat choruses, skip verses, add a tag or build a bridge.

- Before rehearsal, ask the worship leader or tech director how each song will go.
- Come to Wednesday rehearsal if you can. It's the best way to learn the flow.
- During the song, follow the worship leader, not the slide order. If you're in the wrong spot, click to the right slide. Nobody in the room minds.

## Clicking songs

- **Click on the last word of the line** being sung, so the next line is up before anyone needs it.
- **Use the section letters.** Each section (verse, chorus, bridge, tag) starts with a small orange letter on its first slide. Press that letter to jump straight to it when the band goes somewhere unexpected.
- Before service, keep the **welcome loop** running while people arrive.

## Clicking the sermon

The opposite of songs: **wait for the verbal cue.** Click after the pastor makes the point, not before. An early click gives the point away.

## The three clicks that matter

1. **The first worship slide** starts the service. It changes the lights, looks and camera together.
2. **The first sermon slide** switches to the message look.
3. **The last slide** ends the livestream. Click it only when the service is over.
