The pastor's manuscript becomes slides between Thursday and Friday. Sermon slides are finished before Sunday morning, always.

## How the manuscript arrives

Usually on Thursday, the pastor sends a Google Doc with the full manuscript. **Anything highlighted in red should become a slide.** Most of it is Scripture. The rest is quotes, lists, takeaways and images.

The highlighting isn't always exact. A reference might be missing, or a passage might be paraphrased. Read for what he's going for, and ask if you're not sure.

## Building the slides

1. Download the Google Doc as a PDF and open it on the ProPresenter computer.
2. Work through the red sections in order.
3. **Scripture:** use ProPresenter's Bible feature to pull the passage by reference. When he reads a whole passage, add it all at once. If he changes the wording, edit the slide to match what he'll actually say.
4. **Quotes, lists and takeaways:** build them in the current sermon series theme.
5. **Images and video:** add them and [[troubleshooting#Video won't play or has no sound|test them]].
6. Leave out anything that isn't highlighted.

## Deadline

Done by **Friday**. Saturday is the backup. Sunday morning is for checking slides with the pastor, not building them.

## On Sunday

The pastor checks his slides against what was built during the morning rehearsal window, before 9:45. Make any edits together with whoever's running slides, so you both know where he's going.
