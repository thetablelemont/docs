Lights change on their own during a normal service. ProPresenter slides send cues to **Lightkey** on the Mac mini, and Lightkey changes the stage lighting.

## Before the service

1. Open **Lightkey** before ProPresenter, and open the **Sunday service** project.
2. Double-click the **All On** preset so the stage is lit while people set up.
3. Open ProPresenter. From there, slides control the lights.

## During the service

Nothing to do. For example, clicking the first worship slide sets the worship lighting.

## Manual control

For testing or special events:

1. Open Lightkey on the Mac mini.
2. Click a scene preset to switch looks.
3. Use the faders to adjust individual fixtures.

## Gear

- Three lamp lights on stage, switched on by hand.
- Stage fixtures controlled by Lightkey. See [[gear|Gear]].

## When it breaks

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Lights don't change with slides | Check that Lightkey is running on the Mac mini |
| Wrong look | Choose the right preset in Lightkey by hand |
| Lights stuck | Quit and reopen Lightkey, then reopen the Sunday service project |
