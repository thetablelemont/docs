ProPresenter runs lyrics, Scripture, video and media. During the service, the operator clicks through slides in time with what's happening, and the output goes to the TVs, the confidence monitor and the stream.

## Key ideas

- **Playlist:** the service in order.
- **Presentation:** one song or one sermon, made of slides.
- **Arrangement:** the order a song's sections play in. Ours often differ from the recording.
- **Look:** which screens show what, and in what style.
- **Stage display:** what the band and pastor see on the confidence monitor.
- **Macro:** a bundle of actions fired by one slide. See [[propresenter-setup|ProPresenter setup]].
